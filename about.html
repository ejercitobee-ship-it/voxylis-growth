Growth is won and lost in conversations.

Voxylis Growth started from a simple belief. Growing companies lose more deals to slow, missed, and mishandled conversations than to almost anything else. A lead goes cold because no one followed up in time. A customer drifts away because a call went unanswered. A team gets so buried in the phone that the real work slows down.

We exist to close that gap. We bring together elite voice talent from the Philippines with the training, structure, and care to turn everyday conversations into growth. Our people are warm, sharp, and genuinely invested in the brands they represent.

We are not a faceless vendor. We are a growth partner that plugs into your team and treats your customers as if they were our own. When you grow, we grow with you.

Meet the founder.

Belle DunnFounder and CEO

Belle founded Voxylis Growth on one simple belief: great businesses deserve great people, and great people deserve meaningful work.

Originally from the Philippines and now based in Chicago, she brings more than eight years in customer service, virtual administration, sales support, operations, and technology driven solutions. She has worked side by side with business owners and teams to keep operations organized, responsive, and moving forward.

Working remotely herself taught her what truly makes a distributed team succeed. It is never just about finding someone to complete a task. It is about finding the right person, building clear systems, earning trust, communicating well, and giving people the structure to perform at a high level.

That is the difference you feel here. Outsourcing with Voxylis Growth never means handing tasks to a faraway team. Done right, it becomes a true extension of your business, with the quality, communication, and accountability you expect from people in your own office.

Build better businesses, create better opportunities, and prove that distance should never be a barrier to exceptional work.
